Moola & Gayatri Mantras

Core invocations recited 108 times for spiritual focus, peace, and inner resolve.

Ayyappa Gayatri Mantra

ॐ भूतनाथाय विद्महे भवपुत्राय धीमहि ।
तन्नः शास्ता प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Bhootanaathaya Vidmahe Bhavaputraya Dheemahi, Tannah Sastha Prachodayaat
Om, let us meditate on the Lord of all beings. May the divine son of Lord Shiva illuminate our intellect and guide our spiritual path.

Moola Mantra

ॐ ह्रीं श्रीं क्लीं श्रीं धर्मशास्त्रा नमः ॥
Om Hreem Shreem Kleem Shreem Dharma Shaastraa Namah
The potent seed syllable (Beeja) mantra for Dharma Sastha invoking protection and auspiciousness.

Hariharaputra Gayatri

ॐ हरिहरपुत्राय विद्महे शरवणपुत्राय धीमहि ।
तन्नः शास्ता प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Hariharaputraya Vidmahe Saravanaputraya Dheemahi, Tannah Sastha Prachodayaat
Salutations to the son of Vishnu and Shiva; may Lord Sastha enlighten and protect us.

Kshipra Prasada Mantra

ॐ क्षिप्रप्रसादाय नमः ॥
Om Kshipraprasaadaaya Namah
Salutations to the Lord who grants blessings and grace swiftly to true seekers.

Dhyana Mantra

ध्यायेद् उमापति-रमापति-भाग्यपुत्रं
शान्तं प्रभाकर-सहस्र-समान-भासम् ।
Dhyayed Umapati Ramapati Bhagya Putram, Shaantam Prabhakara Sahasra Samaana Bhaasam
Meditate on the blessed son of Shiva and Vishnu, luminous as a thousand suns and ever peaceful.

Daily Invocations & Nama Mantras

Simple and profound salutations to begin morning and evening prayers.

Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa

சுவாமியே சரணம் ஐயப்பா
சுவாமியே சரணம் ஐயப்பா
Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa
The quintessential call of complete surrender: “O Lord Ayyappa, You alone are my eternal refuge.”

Maha Mantra

ॐ श्री हरिहरपुत्राय नमः ॥
Om Sri Hariharaputraya Namah
Salutations to Sri Ayyappa, the sacred divine offspring of Hari (Vishnu) and Hara (Shiva).

Sastha Mantra

ॐ धर्मशास्त्रे नमः ॥
Om Dharma Sasthre Namah
Salutations to Dharma Sastha, the supreme protector and upholder of cosmic righteousness.

Protection Mantra

ॐ भूतनाथाय नमः ॥
Om Bhootanathaya Namah
Salutations to the Lord of the elements and all living beings.

Grace Mantra

ॐ लोकवीराय नमः ॥
Om Lokaveeraya Namah
Salutations to the heroic protector of the universe.

Peace & Surrender Mantra

ॐ शरणागतवत्सलाय नमः ॥
Om Saranagata Vatsalaya Namah
Salutations to the compassionate Lord who warmly embraces all who seek refuge.

Arogya (Health) Mantra

ॐ महाशास्त्रा वैद्याय नमः ॥
Om Mahasasthree Vaidyaya Namah
Salutations to the Supreme Healer who cures the illnesses of body, mind, and samsara.

Classical Stotrams & Ashtakams

Potent Sanskrit slokas composed for evening Aarti and temple recitations.

Lokaveeram Slokam

लोकवीरं महापूज्यं सर्वरक्षाकरं विभुम् ।
पार्वतीहृदयानन्दं शास्तारं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Lokaveeram Maha Poojyam, Sarva Rakshakaram Vibhum
Parvathi Hridayanandam, Sastharam Pranamaamyaham
I bow to Lord Sastha, the hero of the worlds, deeply revered, all-protecting, omnipresent, and the supreme joy of Mother Parvati's heart.

Viprapoojyam Verse

विप्रपूज्यं विश्ववन्द्यं विष्णुशम्भोः प्रियं सुतम् ।
क्षिप्रप्रसादनिरतं शास्तारं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Viprapoojyam Viswavandyam, Vishnu Sambhoh Priyam Sutam
Kshipra Prasada Niratham, Sastharam Pranamaamyaham
I revere Lord Sastha, worshipped by the wise, revered by the cosmos, dear son of Vishnu and Shiva, who bestows quick grace.

Matha Mathanga Gamanam

मातामातङ्गगमनं कारुण्यामृतपूरितम् ।
सर्वविघ्नहरं देवं शास्तारं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Matha Mathanga Gamanam, Karunyamrutha Pooritham
Sarva Vigna Haram Devam, Sastharam Pranamaamyaham
I bow to Lord Sastha, whose majestic gait is like that of a mighty elephant, who is filled with the nectar of compassion, and who removes every obstacle.

Asmad Kuleshwaram Verse

अस्मत्कुलैश्वरं देवं अस्मच्छत्रुविनाशनम् ।
अस्मदिष्टप्रदातारं शास्तारं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Asmad Kuleshwaram Devam, Asmad Shathru Vinasanam
Asmad Ishta Pradha Daram, Sastharam Pranamaamyaham
I bow to Lord Sastha, the guardian of our lineage, destroyer of our enemies, and granter of our cherished wishes.

Pandyesha Vamsa Thilakam

पाण्ड्येशवंशतिलकं केरलकेलिविग्रहम् ।
आर्तत्राणपरं देवं शास्तारं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Pandyesha Vamsa Thilakam, Kerale Keli Vigraham
Artha Thrana Param Devam, Sastharam Pranamaamyaham
I bow to Lord Sastha, the jewel of the Pandya lineage, who sports in Kerala and is the supreme protector of those in distress.

Traymbaka Puradheesam

त्र्यम्बकपुराधीशं गणाधिपसमन्वितम् ।
गजारूढमहं वन्दे शास्तारं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Traymbaka Puradheesam, Ganadhipa Samanvitham
Gajaroodam Aham Vande, Sastharam Pranamaamyaham
I bow to Lord Sastha, Lord of the city of Trayambaka, accompanied by Lord Ganadhipa, and mounted upon an elephant.

Siva Veerya Samudbhootham

शिववीर्यसमुद्भूतं श्रीनिवासतनोद्भवम् ।
शिखिवाहानुजं वन्दे शास्तारं प्रणमाम्यहम् ॥
Siva Veerya Samudbhootham, Sreenivasa Thanudbhavam
Siki Vahanujam Vande, Sastharam Pranamaamyaham
I bow to Lord Sastha, born of Shiva's divine energy and Srinivasa, and the younger brother of the peacock-mounted Lord Muruga.

Dhanwanthari Mata Verse

यस्य धन्वन्तरिर्माता पिता देवो महेश्वरः ।
तं शास्तारमहं वन्दे महारोगनिवारणम् ॥
Yasya Danwantharir Matha, Pitha Devo Maheswara
Tam Sastharamaham Vande, Maha Roga Nivaranam
I bow to Lord Sastha, whose mother is Dhanwanthari and whose father is Lord Maheswara, the great remover of disease.

Sastha Dhyana Slokam

आश्यामकोमलविशालतनोर्विचित्रं
वासोवसानमरुणोत्पलदामहस्तम् ।
उत्तुङ्गरत्नमकुटं कुटिलाग्रकेशं
शास्तारमिष्टवरदं शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥
Ashyama Komala Vishala Thanum Vichithram
Vasoavasana Arunothphala Dama Hastham
Uthunga Rathna Magudam, Kutilagra Kesam
Sastharam Ishta Varadam Saranam Prapadhye
I take refuge in Lord Sastha, dark, tender, and majestic, adorned in beautiful garments with a red lotus garland in hand, crowned with lofty jewels and having curling locks.

Maha Sastha Namaskaram

भूतनाथ सदानन्द सर्वभूतदयापर ।
रक्ष मां करुणासिन्धो पूर्णचन्द्रनिभानन ॥
Bhootanatha Sadananda, Sarvabhoota Dayapara
Raksha Maam Karunasindho, Poornachandra Nibhaanana
O Lord of beings, ever blissful, full of mercy to all, ocean of compassion with a moon-like face, protect me.

Kshamapanam (Forgiveness)

अपराधसहस्राणि क्रियन्तेऽहर्निशं मया ।
दासोऽयमिति मां मत्वा क्षमस्व परमेश्वर ॥
Aparadha Sahasrani Kriyante Aharnisam Maya
Daso Ayamiti Maam Matva Kshamasva Parameshwara
Thousands of faults are committed by me day and night. Knowing me as your servant, forgive me, O Supreme Lord.

Harivarasanam

The sacred lullaby sung every night at Sabarimala before closing the sanctum.

Harivarasanam – Hariharatmajam

Harivarasanam Viswamohanam Haridadheeswaram Aaradhyapadukam Arivimardhanam Nithyanarthanam Hariharatmajam Devamashraye Sharanakirtanam Bakhtamanasam Bharanalolupam Narthanalasam Arunabhasuram Bhoothanayakam Hariharatmajam Devamashraye Pranayasatyakam Praananayakam Pranathakalpakam Suprabhanjitham Pranayamaparam Yogiparayanam Hariharatmajam Devamashraye Tharunaramasam Vanamohitham Narahareeswaram Saranagamam Karunyalayam Gurumayikavaram Hariharatmajam Devamashraye Sharanamaparam Thamabhakthim Karunyamrithapooritham Sharanamashraye Nithyanirtharam Hariharatmajam Devamashraye Sharanamashraye Nithyanirtharam Hariharatmajam Devamashraye
Swami Saranam Ayyappa
This divine lullaby is sung every night at Sabarimala as the final ritual before the sanctum closes. Devotees believe that Lord Ayyappa goes to rest after hearing Harivarasanam.
