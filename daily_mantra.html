Daily Puja Mantra Book

A Complete Daily Practice

English transliteration edition — dhyana verses, moola mantras, and gayatris for seven deities, in the traditional order of daily worship.

Ganesha Shiva Murugan Vishnu Anjaneya Ayyappa Kubera Aarti Shanti Namaskaram

Twenty-two invocations for a complete puja

Read each verse slowly in its recommended order, or select individual deities for a shorter practice. Every mantra below follows the sequence in the Daily Puja Mantra Book.

Ganesha

✿Remover of obstacles
01

Ganesha Dhyana

शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥
Shuklambharadharam Vishnum, Shashivarnam Chaturbhujam
Prasanna Vadanam Dhyayet, Sarva Vighnopashantaye

Meditate on the Lord clad in white, moon-hued, four-armed, with a serene countenance — for the pacification of all obstacles.

02

Ganesha Moola Mantra

ॐ गं गणपतये नमः ॥
Om Gam Ganapataye Namah

Salutations to Lord Ganapati, remover of obstacles and lord of new beginnings.

03

Ganesha Gayatri

ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे
वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Tatpurushaya Vidmahe, Vakratundaya Dhimahi
Tanno Dantih Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Supreme Being; may the curved-trunked Lord illumine our understanding.

Shiva

✿Source of stillness

Sri Rudram is traditionally learned with correct Vedic svara and pronunciation from a qualified teacher. The opening verse and Rudra Gayatri below are the portions commonly recited in daily devotion.

04

Sri Rudram — Opening

ॐ नमस्ते अस्तु भगवन् विश्वेश्वराय महादेवाय ।
त्र्यम्बकाय त्रिपुरान्तकाय त्रिकाग्निकालाय कालाग्निरुद्राय ।
नीलकण्ठाय मृत्युञ्जयाय सर्वेश्वराय सदाशिवाय श्रीमन्महादेवाय नमः ॥
Om Namaste Astu Bhagavan, Vishveshvaraya Mahadevaya
Tryambakaya Tripurantakaya, Trikagnikalaya Kalagnirudraya
Nilakanthaya Mrityunjayaya, Sarveshvaraya Sadashivaya, Shriman Mahadevaya Namah

Salutations to you, O Lord of the universe, the great God — three-eyed destroyer of the triple cities, blue-throated conqueror of death, eternal all-lord Sadashiva.

05

Shiva Panchakshara

ॐ नमः शिवाय ॥
Om Namah Shivaya

Salutations to Shiva, the auspicious one, the source of inner stillness.

06

Rudra Gayatri

ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे
महादेवाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो रुद्रः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Tatpurushaya Vidmahe, Mahadevaya Dhimahi
Tanno Rudrah Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Supreme Being; may Lord Rudra illumine our understanding.

Murugan

✿The six-faced lord
07

Murugan Moola Mantra

ॐ सरवणभवाय नमः ॥
Om Saravanabhavaya Namah

Salutations to Murugan, born amid the sacred Saravana reeds.

08

Shanmukha Gayatri

ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे
महासेनाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो षण्मुखः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Tatpurushaya Vidmahe, Mahasenaya Dhimahi
Tanno Shanmukhah Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Supreme Being; may the six-faced Lord illumine our understanding.

Vishnu

✿Sustainer of the worlds
09

Vishnu Dhyana

शान्ताकारं भुजगशयनं पद्मनाभं सुरेशं
विश्वाधारं गगनसदृशं मेघवर्णं शुभाङ्गम् ।
लक्ष्मीकान्तं कमलनयनं योगिभिर्ध्यानगम्यं
वन्दे विष्णुं भवभयहरं सर्वलोकैकनाथम् ॥
Shantakaram Bhujagashayanam, Padmanabham Suresham
Vishvadharam Gaganasadrisham, Meghavarnam Shubhangam
Lakshmikantam Kamalanayanam, Yogibhirdhyanagamyam
Vande Vishnum Bhavabhayaharam, Sarvalokaikanatham

I bow to Vishnu, peaceful of form, reclining on the serpent, lotus-naveled, sustainer of the universe, sky-hued, beloved of Lakshmi, reachable by yogis through meditation, remover of worldly fear, sole lord of all worlds.

10

Vishnu Ashtakshara

ॐ नमो नारायणाय ॥
Om Namo Narayanaya

Salutations to Narayana, the all-pervading Lord.

11

Vishnu Gayatri

ॐ नारायणाय विद्महे
वासुदेवाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो विष्णुः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Narayanaya Vidmahe, Vasudevaya Dhimahi
Tanno Vishnuh Prachodayat

Let us meditate on Narayana; may Lord Vishnu illumine our understanding.

Anjaneya

✿Courage and devoted service
12

Anjaneya Dhyana

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं
जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम् ।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं
श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥
Manojavam Maruta Tulyavegam, Jitendriyam Buddhimatam Varishtham
Vatatmajam Vanarayuthamukhyam, Sri Rama Dutam Sharanam Prapadye

I take refuge in Hanuman, swift as thought and wind, master of the senses, foremost among the wise, son of the wind-god, chief of the monkey clan, and messenger of Sri Rama.

13

Hanuman Moola Mantra

ॐ हनुमते नमः ॥
Om Hanumate Namah

Salutations to Hanuman, embodiment of courage, strength, and devotion.

14

Hanuman Gayatri

ॐ अञ्जनेयाय विद्महे
वायुपुत्राय धीमहि ।
तन्नो हनुमान् प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Anjaneyaya Vidmahe, Vayuputraya Dhimahi
Tanno Hanuman Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the son of Anjana; may Hanuman, son of the wind, illumine our understanding.

Ayyappa

✿Lord of Sabarimala
15

Ayyappa Saranam

சுவாமியே சரணம் ஐயப்பா
Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa

A complete offering of the heart to Lord Ayyappa.

16

Ayyappa / Shasta Gayatri

ॐ भूतनाथाय विद्महे
भवपुत्राय धीमहि ।
तन्नः शास्ता प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Bhootanathaya Vidmahe, Bhavaputraya Dhimahi
Tanno Shasta Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Lord of all beings; may the divine son of Shiva illumine our path.

Kubera

✿Optional
17

Kubera Mantra

ॐ राजाधिराजाय प्रसह्य साहिने नमो वयं वैश्रवणाय कुर्महे ।
स मे कामान् कामकामाय मह्यं कामेश्वरो वैश्रवणो ददातु ॥
कुबेराय वैश्रवणाय महाराजाय नमः ॥
Om Rajadhirajaya Prasahya Sahine, Namo Vayam Vaishravanaya Kurmahe
Sa Me Kaman Kamakamaya Mahyam, Kameshvaro Vaishravano Dadatu
Kuberaya Vaishravanaya Maharajaya Namah

We bow to Vaishravana (Kubera), king of kings, lord of wealth and abundance; may he, the granter of desires, fulfill our righteous wishes.

Aarti, Peace & Offering

✿Closing the practice
18

Aarti — Shiva

कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं संसारसारं भुजगेन्द्रहारम् ।
सदा वसन्तं हृदयारविन्दे भवं भवानीसहितं नमामि ॥
Karpura Gauram Karunavataram, Samsara Saram Bhujagendra Haram
Sada Vasantam Hridayaravinde, Bhavam Bhavani Sahitam Namami

I salute Shiva, white as camphor, the embodiment of compassion, essence of worldly existence, adorned with the serpent-king, who dwells eternally in the lotus of the heart, together with Bhavani.

19

Sarva Mangala Prayer

सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके ।
शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
Sarva Mangala Mangalye, Shive Sarvartha Sadhike
Sharanye Tryambake Gauri, Narayani Namostute

Salutations to you, O Narayani — the auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, the accomplisher of all goals, the refuge of all, three-eyed Gauri.

20

Universal Peace

लोकाः समस्ताः सुखिनो भवन्तु ॥
Lokah Samastah Sukhino Bhavantu (recited three times)

May all beings everywhere be happy and free.

21

Shanti

ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥
Om Shantih Shantih Shantih

May peace pervade the body, the mind, and the world around us — a threefold peace for all realms of being.

22

Final Offering — Namaskaram

कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा बुद्ध्यात्मना वा प्रकृतेः स्वभावात् ।
करोमि यद्यत् सकलं परस्मै नारायणायेति समर्पयामि ॥
Kayena Vacha Manasendriyairva, Buddhyatmana Va Prakriteh Svabhavat
Karomi Yadyat Sakalam Parasmai, Narayanayeti Samarpayami

Whatever I do with body, speech, mind, senses, intellect, or by the very nature of my being — all of it I offer to the Supreme, unto Narayana.

The 15–20 minute daily routine

The full sequence from the Daily Puja Mantra Book, in the traditional recommended order.

01 · Ganesha

Dhyana + Moola Mantra

02 · Shiva

Rudram opening + Om Namah Shivaya + Rudra Gayatri

03 · Murugan

Om Saravanabhavaya Namah + Shanmukha Gayatri

04 · Vishnu

Shantakaram + Om Namo Narayanaya + Vishnu Gayatri

05 · Anjaneya

Manojavam + Om Hanumate Namah + Hanuman Gayatri

06 · Ayyappa

Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa + Ayyappa Gayatri

07 · Kubera

Optional mantra for abundance

08 · Aarti

Karpura Gauram, offered with a lit lamp

09 · Universal Peace

Lokah Samastah Sukhino Bhavantu

10 · Shanti

Om Shantih Shantih Shantih

11 · Namaskaram

Kayena Vacha — the final offering
