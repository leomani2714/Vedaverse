Daily Puja Mantra Book

A Complete Daily Practice

English transliteration edition — dhyana verses, moola mantras, and gayatris for eleven deities, in the traditional order of daily worship.

Ganesha Shiva Murugan Vishnu Devi Lakshmi Saraswati Surya Anjaneya Ayyappa Kubera Aarti Shanti Namaskaram

Forty-six invocations for a complete puja

Read each verse slowly in its recommended order, or select individual deities for a shorter practice. Every mantra below follows the sequence in the Daily Puja Mantra Book, and each deity now carries its own dhyana (meditation verse), moola mantra, and gayatri — just like the Shiva section.

Ganesha

✿Remover of obstacles
01

Ganesha Dhyana

Shuklambharadharam Vishnum, Shashivarnam Chaturbhujam
Prasanna Vadanam Dhyayet, Sarva Vighnopashantaye

Meditate on the Lord clad in white, moon-hued, four-armed, with a serene countenance — for the pacification of all obstacles.

02

Ganesha Moola Mantra

Om Gam Ganapataye Namah

Salutations to Lord Ganapati, remover of obstacles and lord of new beginnings.

03

Ganesha Gayatri

Om Tatpurushaya Vidmahe, Vakratundaya Dhimahi
Tanno Dantih Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Supreme Being; may the curved-trunked Lord illumine our understanding.

Shiva

✿Source of stillness

Sri Rudram is traditionally learned with correct Vedic svara and pronunciation from a qualified teacher. The opening verse and Rudra Gayatri below are the portions commonly recited in daily devotion.

04

Sri Rudram — Opening

Om Namaste Astu Bhagavan, Vishveshvaraya Mahadevaya
Tryambakaya Tripurantakaya, Trikagnikalaya Kalagnirudraya
Nilakanthaya Mrityunjayaya, Sarveshvaraya Sadashivaya, Shriman Mahadevaya Namah

Salutations to you, O Lord of the universe, the great God — three-eyed destroyer of the triple cities, blue-throated conqueror of death, eternal all-lord Sadashiva.

05

Shiva Panchakshara

Om Namah Shivaya

Salutations to Shiva, the auspicious one, the source of inner stillness.

06

Rudra Gayatri

Om Tatpurushaya Vidmahe, Mahadevaya Dhimahi
Tanno Rudrah Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Supreme Being; may Lord Rudra illumine our understanding.

Murugan

✿The six-faced lord
07

Murugan Dhyana

Shadananam Kumkuma Raktavarnam, Mahamatim Divya Mayura Vahanam
Rudrasya Sunum Sura-Sainya-Natham, Guham Sadaham Sharanam Prapadye

I ever take refuge in Guha (Murugan) — six-faced, vermilion-hued, of great wisdom, mounted on the divine peacock, son of Rudra, and commander of the celestial army.

08

Murugan Moola Mantra

Om Saravanabhavaya Namah

Salutations to Murugan, born amid the sacred Saravana reeds.

09

Shanmukha Gayatri

Om Tatpurushaya Vidmahe, Mahasenaya Dhimahi
Tanno Shanmukhah Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Supreme Being; may the six-faced Lord illumine our understanding.

Vishnu

✿Sustainer of the worlds
10

Vishnu Dhyana

Shantakaram Bhujagashayanam, Padmanabham Suresham
Vishvadharam Gaganasadrisham, Meghavarnam Shubhangam
Lakshmikantam Kamalanayanam, Yogibhirdhyanagamyam
Vande Vishnum Bhavabhayaharam, Sarvalokaikanatham

I bow to Vishnu, peaceful of form, reclining on the serpent, lotus-naveled, sustainer of the universe, sky-hued, beloved of Lakshmi, reachable by yogis through meditation, remover of worldly fear, sole lord of all worlds.

11

Vishnu Ashtakshara

Om Namo Narayanaya

Salutations to Narayana, the all-pervading Lord.

12

Vishnu Gayatri

Om Narayanaya Vidmahe, Vasudevaya Dhimahi
Tanno Vishnuh Prachodayat

Let us meditate on Narayana; may Lord Vishnu illumine our understanding.

Devi

✿The Divine Mother
13

Devi Dhyana

Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu, Shakti-Rupena Samsthita
Namastasyai Namastasyai, Namastasyai Namo Namah

To that Goddess who dwells in all beings in the form of power (shakti) — salutations to Her, salutations to Her, salutations to Her, again and again.

14

Durga Moola Mantra

Om Dum Durgayai Namah

Salutations to Durga, the Goddess who carries Her devotees beyond every difficulty.

15

Durga Gayatri

Om Kaatyayanaya Vidmahe, Kanyakumari Dhimahi
Tanno Durgih Prachodayat

Let us meditate on Katyayani; may the ever-youthful Goddess Durga illumine our understanding.

Lakshmi

✿Goddess of abundance
16

Lakshmi Dhyana

Namastestu Mahamaye, Shripithe Sura-Pujite
Shankha-Chakra-Gada-Haste, Mahalakshmi Namostute

Salutations to You, O great illusive power, seated on the throne of glory and worshipped by the gods, bearing the conch, discus, and mace — salutations to Mahalakshmi.

17

Lakshmi Moola Mantra

Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah

Salutations to Mahalakshmi, giver of prosperity, grace, and wellbeing.

18

Lakshmi Gayatri

Om Mahadevyai Cha Vidmahe, Vishnu-Patnyai Cha Dhimahi
Tanno Lakshmih Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the great Goddess, consort of Vishnu; may Lakshmi illumine our understanding.

Saraswati

✿Goddess of learning
19

Saraswati Dhyana

Ya Kundendu-Tushara-Hara-Dhavala, Ya Shubhra-Vastravrita
Ya Vina-Vara-Danda-Mandita-Kara, Ya Shveta-Padmasana
Ya Brahmachyuta-Shankara-Prabhritibhih, Devaih Sada Vandita
Sa Mam Patu Sarasvati Bhagavati, Nihshesha-Jadyapaha

She who is white as jasmine, moon, snow, or a garland of pearls; clothed in white; whose hand is graced by the fine veena; who sits on a white lotus; who is ever worshipped by Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and the gods — may that Goddess Saraswati protect me and remove all dullness of mind.

20

Saraswati Moola Mantra

Om Aim Saraswatyai Namah

Salutations to Saraswati, Goddess of speech, wisdom, and the arts.

21

Saraswati Gayatri

Om Vagdevyai Cha Vidmahe, Kamarajaya Dhimahi
Tanno Devi Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Goddess of speech; may She illumine our understanding.

Surya

✿The sun, giver of life
22

Surya Dhyana

Japakusuma Sankasham, Kashyapeyam Mahadyutim
Tamorim Sarva-Papaghnam, Pranatosmi Divakaram

I bow to Divakara (the Sun), radiant like the hibiscus flower, descendant of Kashyapa, of great brilliance, the enemy of darkness, and destroyer of all sins.

23

Surya Moola Mantra

Om Ghrinih Suryaya Namah

Salutations to Surya, the radiant sun who sustains all life.

24

Surya Gayatri

Om Bhaskaraya Vidmahe, Divakaraya Dhimahi
Tanno Suryah Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the maker of light; may the Sun illumine our understanding.

Anjaneya

✿Courage and devoted service
25

Anjaneya Dhyana

Manojavam Maruta Tulyavegam, Jitendriyam Buddhimatam Varishtham
Vatatmajam Vanarayuthamukhyam, Sri Rama Dutam Sharanam Prapadye

I take refuge in Hanuman, swift as thought and wind, master of the senses, foremost among the wise, son of the wind-god, chief of the monkey clan, and messenger of Sri Rama.

26

Hanuman Moola Mantra

Om Hanumate Namah

Salutations to Hanuman, embodiment of courage, strength, and devotion.

27

Hanuman Gayatri

Om Anjaneyaya Vidmahe, Vayuputraya Dhimahi
Tanno Hanuman Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the son of Anjana; may Hanuman, son of the wind, illumine our understanding.

Ayyappa

✿Lord of Sabarimala
28

Ayyappa Dhyana — Pancharatnam

Lokaviram Mahapujyam, Sarva-Rakshakaram Vibhum
Parvati Hridayanandam, Shastaram Pranamamyaham || 1 ||

Vipra Pujyam Vishva Vandyam, Vishnu-Shambhoh Priyam Sutam
Kshipra Prasada Niratam, Shastaram Pranamamyaham || 2 ||

Matta Matanga Gamanam, Karunyamrita Puritam
Sarva Vighna Haram Devam, Shastaram Pranamamyaham || 3 ||

Asmat Kuleshvaram Devam, Asmach-Chatru Vinashanam
Asmad Ishta Pradataram, Shastaram Pranamamyaham || 4 ||

Pandyesha Vamsha Tilakam, Kerale Keli Vigraham
Arta Trana Param Devam, Shastaram Pranamamyaham || 5 ||

Pancharatnakhyam Etad Yo, Nityam Shuddhah Pathennarah
Tasya Prasanno Bhagavan, Shasta Vasati Manase

Five gems in praise of Shasta (Ayyappa): (1) I salute the hero of the worlds, greatly worshipped, protector of all, the delight of Parvati's heart. (2) I salute Him who is honoured by the wise and revered by the universe, the beloved son of Vishnu and Shiva, ever intent on swiftly bestowing grace. (3) I salute the god whose gait is like a rutting elephant's, filled with the nectar of compassion, remover of all obstacles. (4) I salute Him, the lord of our lineage, destroyer of our foes, granter of our desires. (5) I salute the god who is the jewel of the Pandya line, who took a playful form in Kerala, the supreme protector of the distressed. Whoever recites these five gems daily with purity — Bhagavan Shasta, well pleased, comes to dwell in that person's heart.

29

Ayyappa Saranam

Swamiye Saranam Ayyappa

A complete offering of the heart to Lord Ayyappa.

30

Ayyappa / Shasta Gayatri

Om Bhootanathaya Vidmahe, Bhavaputraya Dhimahi
Tanno Shasta Prachodayat

Let us meditate on the Lord of all beings; may the divine son of Shiva illumine our path.

Kubera

✿Optional
31

Kubera Prayer Verse

Om Rajadhirajaya Prasahya Sahine, Namo Vayam Vaishravanaya Kurmahe
Sa Me Kaman Kamakamaya Mahyam, Kameshvaro Vaishravano Dadatu
Kuberaya Vaishravanaya Maharajaya Namah

We bow to Vaishravana (Kubera), king of kings, lord of wealth and abundance; may he, the granter of desires, fulfill our righteous wishes.

32

Kubera Moola Mantra

Om Kuberaya Namah

Salutations to Kubera, keeper of the treasury of the gods and lord of material wellbeing.

33

Kubera Gayatri

Om Vaishravanaya Vidmahe, Rajarajaya Dhimahi
Tanno Kuberah Prachodayat

Let us meditate on Vaishravana; may Kubera, king among kings, illumine our understanding.

Additional Daily Shlokas

✿Optional additions
34

Karaagre Vasate Lakshmi

Karagre Vasate Lakshmih, Karamadhye Sarasvati
Karamoole Tu Govindah, Prabhate Karadarshanam

At the fingertips dwells Lakshmi, at the middle of the hand Saraswati, and at its base Govinda. At dawn, I behold my hands with reverence.

35

Samudra Vasane Devi

Samudra Vasane Devi, Parvata-Stana-Mandale
Vishnu-Patni Namastubhyam, Pada-Sparsham Kshamasva Me

O Goddess Earth, clothed by the ocean and adorned with mountains, consort of Vishnu, forgive me for touching you with my feet.

36

Guru Vandana

Gurur Brahma Gurur Vishnuh, Gurur Devo Maheshvarah
Guruh Sakshat Param Brahma, Tasmai Shri Gurave Namah

The guru is Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshvara; the guru is the Supreme Reality itself. Salutations to that revered teacher.

37

Deepa Jyoti

Shubham Karoti Kalyanam, Arogyam Dhana-Sampadah
Shatru-Buddhi-Vinashaya, Deepa-Jyotir Namostute

Salutations to the light of the lamp, which brings auspiciousness, wellbeing, prosperity, and the destruction of hostile thoughts.

Easy Shlokas

✿Simple daily prayers
38

Om Namah Shivaya

Om Namah Shivaya

Salutations to Lord Shiva. Repeat softly with a calm and steady mind.

39

Rama Nama

Sri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram

A simple prayer celebrating the name of Lord Rama, the ideal of courage and righteousness.

40

Krishna Prayer

Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna Hare Hare
Hare Rama Hare Rama, Rama Rama Hare Hare

A joyful remembrance of Krishna and Rama, suitable for singing or quiet repetition.

41

Asato Ma

Asato Ma Sadgamaya, Tamaso Ma Jyotirgamaya
Mrityor Ma Amritam Gamaya

Lead me from the unreal to truth, from darkness to light, and from mortality to immortality.

Aarti, Peace & Offering

✿Closing the practice
42

Aarti — Shiva

Karpura Gauram Karunavataram, Samsara Saram Bhujagendra Haram
Sada Vasantam Hridayaravinde, Bhavam Bhavani Sahitam Namami

I salute Shiva, white as camphor, the embodiment of compassion, essence of worldly existence, adorned with the serpent-king, who dwells eternally in the lotus of the heart, together with Bhavani.

43

Sarva Mangala Prayer

Sarva Mangala Mangalye, Shive Sarvartha Sadhike
Sharanye Tryambake Gauri, Narayani Namostute

Salutations to you, O Narayani — the auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, the accomplisher of all goals, the refuge of all, three-eyed Gauri.

44

Universal Peace

Lokah Samastah Sukhino Bhavantu (recited three times)

May all beings everywhere be happy and free.

45

Shanti

Om Shantih Shantih Shantih

May peace pervade the body, the mind, and the world around us — a threefold peace for all realms of being.

46

Final Offering — Namaskaram

Kayena Vacha Manasendriyairva, Buddhyatmana Va Prakriteh Svabhavat
Karomi Yadyat Sakalam Parasmai, Narayanayeti Samarpayami

Whatever I do with body, speech, mind, senses, intellect, or by the very nature of my being — all of it I offer to the Supreme, unto Narayana.
