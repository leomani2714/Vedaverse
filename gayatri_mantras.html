ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः

Explore the mantras

Prayers for clarity, courage, wisdom, and devotion.

Gayatri Mantra

ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः । तत्सवितुः वरेण्यं । भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि । धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Bhūr Bhuvaḥ Svaḥ, Tat Savitur Vareṇyaṃ, Bhargo Devasya Dhīmahi, Dhiyo Yo Naḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May the divine light of Savitṛ guide our intellect and illuminate our inner wisdom.

Shiva Gayatri

ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे महादेवाय धीमहि तन्नो रुद्रः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Tatpuruṣāya Vidmahe Mahādevāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Rudraḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Rudra inspire and awaken our mind with divine strength and clarity.

Vishnu Gayatri

ॐ नारायणाय विद्महे वासुदेवाय धीमहि तन्नो विष्णुः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Nārāyaṇāya Vidmahe Vāsudevāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Viṣṇuḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Vishnu guide our intellect and protect our path with grace.

Rama Gayatri

ॐ दाशरथाय विद्महे सीतावल्लभाय धीमहि तन्नो रामः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Dāśarathāya Vidmahe Sītāvallabhāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Rāmaḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Rama inspire righteousness, serenity, and wisdom within us.

Krishna Gayatri

ॐ देवकीनन्दनाय विद्महे वासुदेवाय धीमहि तन्नो कृष्णः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Devakīnandanāya Vidmahe Vāsudevāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Kṛṣṇaḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Krishna awaken divine consciousness and inner joy.

Ganesha Gayatri

ॐ एकदन्ताय विद्महे वक्रतुण्डाय धीमहि तन्नो दन्तिः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Ekadantāya Vidmahe Vakratuṇḍāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Dantiḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Ganesha remove obstacles and guide our efforts wisely.

Subrahmanya Gayatri

ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे महासेनाय धीमहि तन्नो षण्मुखः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Tatpuruṣāya Vidmahe Mahāsenāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Ṣaṇmukhaḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Murugan bless us with courage, clarity, and spiritual strength.

Hanuman Gayatri

ॐ अञ्जनेयाय विद्महे वायुपुत्राय धीमहि तन्नो हनुमान् प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Añjaneyāya Vidmahe Vāyuputrāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Hanumān Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Hanuman strengthen our body, mind, and devotion.

Lakshmi Gayatri

ॐ महालक्ष्म्यै विद्महे विष्णुपत्नी धीमहि तन्नो लक्ष्मीः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Mahālakṣmyai Vidmahe Viṣṇupatnyai Dhīmahi, Tanno Lakṣmīḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Lakshmi bless us with prosperity, harmony, and grace.

Saraswati Gayatri

ॐ सरस्वत्यै विद्महे ब्रह्मपत्नी धीमहि तन्नो देवी प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Sarasvatyai Vidmahe Brahmapatnyai Dhīmahi, Tanno Devī Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Saraswati grant knowledge, clarity, and true wisdom.

Durga Gayatri

ॐ कात्यायनाय विद्महे कanyakुमारी धीमहि तन्नो दुर्गा प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Kātyāyanāya Vidmahe Kanyakumārī Dhīmahi, Tanno Durgā Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Durga protect us and inspire courage and strength.

Surya Gayatri

ॐ भास्कराय विद्महे महाद्युतिकराय धीमहि तन्नो आदित्यः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Bhāskarāya Vidmahe Mahādyutikarāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Ādityaḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May the Sun God illuminate our life with health and enlightenment.

Brahma Gayatri

ॐ चतुर्मुखाय विद्महे हंसवाहनाय धीमहि तन्नो ब्रह्मा प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Caturmukhāya Vidmahe Haṃsavāhanāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Brahmā Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Brahma, the four-faced creator, inspire our intellect with divine knowledge and creativity.

Narasimha Gayatri

ॐ वज्रनखाय विद्महे तीक्ष्णदंष्ट्राय धीमहि तन्नो नृसिंहः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Vajranakhāya Vidmahe Tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭrāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Nṛsiṃhaḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Lord Narasimha protect us and illuminate our mind with fearlessness and divine strength.

Radha Gayatri

ॐ वृषभानुजायै विद्महे कृष्णप्रियायै धीमहि तन्नो राधा प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Vṛṣabhānujāyai Vidmahe Kṛṣṇapriyāyai Dhīmahi, Tanno Rādhā Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Radha awaken pure devotion, love, and spiritual bliss within us.

Sita Gayatri

ॐ जानक्यै विद्महे रामप्रियायै धीमहि तन्नो सीता प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Jānakyai Vidmahe Rāmapriyāyai Dhīmahi, Tanno Sītā Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Sita inspire purity, devotion, and steadfast dharma in our lives.

Guru Gayatri

ॐ गुरुदेवाय विद्महे परब्रह्मणे धीमहि तन्नो गुरुः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Gurudevāya Vidmahe Parabrahmaṇe Dhīmahi, Tanno Guruḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May the Guru illuminate our path and guide our intellect toward the Supreme Truth.

Agni Gayatri

ॐ महाज्वालाय विद्महे अग्निदेवाय धीमहि तन्नो अग्निः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Mahājvālāya Vidmahe Agnidevāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Agniḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Agni, the divine fire, purify us and inspire clarity, energy, and transformation.

Chandra Gayatri

ॐ क्षीरपुत्राय विद्महे अमृतात्मने धीमहि तन्नो सोमः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Kṣīraputrāya Vidmahe Amṛtātmane Dhīmahi, Tanno Somaḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Chandra (the Moon) grant us calmness, emotional balance, and nectar-like peace.

Kali Gayatri

ॐ कालिकायै विद्महे श्मशानवासिन्यै धीमहि तन्नो काली प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Kālikāyai Vidmahe Śmaśānavāsinyai Dhīmahi, Tanno Kālī Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Kali destroy fear and negativity, awakening transformative strength and liberation.

Parvati Gayatri

ॐ गिरिजायै विद्महे शिवप्रियायै धीमहि तन्नो दुर्गा प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Girijāyai Vidmahe Śivapriyāyai Dhīmahi, Tanno Durgā Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Parvati (Girija) bless us with strength, devotion, and harmonious power.

Annapurna Gayatri

ॐ भगवत्यै विद्महे महेश्वर्यै धीमहि तन्नो अन्नपूर्णा प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Bhagavatyai Vidmahe Maheśvaryai Dhīmahi, Tanno Annapūrṇā Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Goddess Annapurna nourish us with abundance, contentment, and divine grace.

Garuda Gayatri

ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे सुवर्णपक्षाय धीमहि तन्नो गरुडः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Tatpuruṣāya Vidmahe Suvarṇapakṣāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Garuḍaḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Garuda grant speed, protection, and the power to rise above obstacles.

Indra Gayatri

ॐ सहस्राक्षाय विद्महे वज्रहस्ताय धीमहि तन्नो इन्द्रः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Sahasrākṣāya Vidmahe Vajrahastāya Dhīmahi, Tanno Indraḥ Prachodayāt.
Meaning: May Indra, the thousand-eyed lord, inspire courage, leadership, and victory over challenges.
